First Matter

Playtest builds. Landscape, touch or mouse.

Latest build

Assemble matter from quarks. Touch a particle to warm it, flick warm particles together to forge a proton, then guide an electron into orbit before the window closes.

Play

On an iPad or phone

Open the link, then Share → Add to Home Screen. It runs fullscreen in landscape with no browser chrome, and loads faster after the first time.

First load is around 40 MB, so use wifi. If a build looks stale, come back to this page — it clears the cache on load.

Controls

Tap a particle to warm it · Swipe through it to flick · Pinch together to survey the sector, release to spring back · Two-finger tap to pin the survey view.
On a keyboard: Tab survey · P pin · S slingshot · Z zen · X sandbox · [ ] time · R restart · F5/F6 previous/next level · F3 debug overlay.
Black holes appear on level 5 ("The Well") — press F6 four times from the start to go straight there.
